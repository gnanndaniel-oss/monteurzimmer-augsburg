The drill bit snaps, you run out of screws, the site is missing a core drill: when you are working away in Augsburg, you often need to get hold of materials, tools or safety gear at short notice. This guide shows which places to buy from, when each one is the right choice and how to plan your shopping so it doesn't eat up half your working day.

We deliberately don't list addresses or opening hours of individual stores – they change, and outdated information helps no one. Check the location and opening hours shortly before you go on the retailer's website or in your map app.

## DIY stores: quick and flexible

DIY stores are the first choice when you need something fast. The big stores are usually on arterial roads or in the area around Augsburg and have large car parks with room for Sprinter vans too. Many have their own sections or checkouts for trade customers, and some offer tool hire at the service desk – handy if you only need a machine for a day.

**Tip:** Many stores open early in the morning. Shopping before work usually goes faster than in the afternoon or on a Saturday.

## Specialist trade wholesalers: for professionals with proof of trade registration

Electricians, plumbers and heating engineers usually buy larger quantities from specialist wholesalers. Prices are generally better for trade customers, the advice is expert, and many wholesalers also deliver straight to the site. As a rule you need a customer account or **proof of trade registration** ("Gewerbenachweis"). If your company is already a customer of a nationwide wholesale chain, it is worth asking whether they have a branch in Augsburg or the surrounding area – your terms will often apply there too.

## Tool and plant hire

Core drill, construction dryer, plate compactor, scaffolding, mini excavator or aerial work platform: you don't buy equipment like this for a single job. Plant hire and machine rental companies in the region rent it out by the day or week, often with delivery and collection.

Clarify before hiring:

1. How much are the **deposit** and insurance?
2. Is it **delivered and collected** – and at what times?
3. Which **driving licence** is needed to transport or operate it?
4. What happens if it **breaks down** on site?
5. How is it **charged** – per day, weekend or week?

## Workwear and safety gear

On longer jobs you will need replacements: gloves, safety glasses, hearing protection, sometimes a new hard hat or safety boots. Try safety boots on in a specialist shop if you can – the wrong size will make you pay for it after ten hours on site. Workwear is easy to reorder online; allow for the delivery time and have it delivered to your accommodation if the landlord allows this – ask first.

## Sundays and after 8 pm: what then?

Bavaria applies the federal Shop Closing Act ("Ladenschlussgesetz"): shops may open **Monday to Saturday from 6 am to 8 pm**, and DIY stores stay closed **on Sundays and public holidays**. There are exceptions for certain outlets, such as at petrol stations and railway stations, but they only carry a very limited range.

How to avoid running short at the weekend:

- **On Friday, check what materials you need for Monday** and get any missing parts on Saturday.
- **Keep a stock of small parts** in the vehicle: screws, wall plugs, bits, cable ties, fuses.
- **Order online** for delivery at the start of the week if it isn't urgent.

## Storing materials safely

Leaving tools in the van overnight is a risk. Check with the client whether there is a lockable container or room on site. Take valuable tools and batteries with you to your accommodation in the evening – you can charge the batteries there too. Please discuss larger quantities of material with the landlord in advance.

## Frequently asked questions about shopping for workers in Augsburg

### Are DIY stores in Augsburg open on Sundays?

No. Bavaria applies the federal Shop Closing Act; DIY stores are closed on Sundays and public holidays. On working days, shops may open from 6 am to 8 pm.

### Where can I hire special tools in Augsburg?

From plant hire and machine rental companies in the region, and in some cases at the service desk of large DIY stores. Clarify the deposit, delivery and billing in advance.

### Do I need proof of trade registration to buy from a wholesaler?

Usually, yes. Specialist wholesalers generally sell to trade customers with a customer account or proof of trade registration.

### Can I store tools in the accommodation?

It is best to take valuable tools and batteries with you to the accommodation in the evening. Please discuss larger quantities of material with the landlord in advance.
