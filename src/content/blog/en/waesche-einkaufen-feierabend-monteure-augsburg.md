## Washing workwear properly

Workwear is often very dirty – oil, grease, cement, dust. A few rules protect both the fabric and the machine:

1. **Shake out and brush off the worst** before the clothes go into the machine. Cement dust and sand don't belong in the drum.
2. **Wash separately:** don't wash workwear together with towels or casual clothes.
3. **Pre-treat stains:** soak oil and grease stains with ox-gall soap ("Gallseife") or stain remover spray.
4. **Check the care label:** high-visibility clothing loses its brightness at the wrong temperature and the reflective strips can be damaged.
5. **Empty your pockets:** screws, bits and pencils damage the drum.

Tip: For longer stays, bring detergent pods with you or buy detergent in the first week at a chemist's/drugstore ("Drogeriemarkt").

## Shopping: opening hours and planning

Bavaria applies the federal Shop Closing Act ("Ladenschlussgesetz"): shops may open **Monday to Saturday from 6 am to 8 pm**, and supermarkets stay closed on **Sundays and public holidays**. There are exceptions for some outlets at railway stations and petrol stations, but they have a small range and higher prices.

You will find discount stores and supermarkets in every district of Augsburg, and there are shops near our accommodation too. Here's how to handle shopping when you're working away:

- **Do the weekly shop on Saturday** – and think ahead to Sunday and Monday morning.
- **Share a shopping list within the team** so not everyone drives there separately.
- **Build up basic supplies:** pasta, rice, tinned food, coffee, spices, oil.
- **Prepare your packed lunch ("Brotzeit") for the site** the evening before to save time in the morning.

All Monteurzimmer Augsburg accommodation has a fully equipped kitchen with a hob, oven, microwave, fridge and crockery. On longer jobs, that makes a big difference.

**Example calculation:** If self-catering saves you just €10 a day compared with snack bars and restaurants, you will have around €280 more in your pocket after four weeks – per person. For a team of six workers, that is over €1,600 a month.

Simple dishes that work well during a working week:

- **Stews and chilli** in large batches – enough for two or three evenings
- **Pasta and rice pan dishes** with vegetables and meat
- **Oven dishes** such as bakes that cook without supervision
