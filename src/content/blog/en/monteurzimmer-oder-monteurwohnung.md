Whether you are an electrician, a plumbing and heating fitter, a drywaller or a service technician: if you are on an installation job in Augsburg for several weeks or months, you spend more time in your accommodation than in some homes. The choice between a workers' room and a workers' apartment therefore affects not only the cost, but also how rested the team is when they turn up on site in the morning.

## The terms: what is what?

- **Workers' room (Monteurzimmer):** A furnished single, double or multi-bed room in accommodation with a shared kitchen and shared bathroom facilities. Charged per person per night.
- **Workers' apartment (Monteurwohnung):** A complete, furnished apartment let exclusively to one team. Charged per apartment per day – regardless of whether every bed is occupied.
- **Studio apartment (Monteurapartment):** A small, self-contained unit for 1 to 4 people.
- **Workers' house (Monteurhaus):** A whole house for larger teams, often with a garden and more parking spaces.

## The comparison at a glance

| Criterion | Workers' room | Workers' apartment |
|---|---|---|
| Ideal team size | 1–3 people | 4 people or more |
| Billing | per person per night | per apartment per day |
| Price at Monteurzimmer Augsburg | from €12–25 per person/night | from €60 (4 people) to €200 (20 people) per day |
| Privacy | own room, kitchen and bathroom may be shared | whole apartment for the team only |
| Flexibility if team size changes | high – beds can be booked individually | lower – price applies to the apartment |
| Kitchen | yes | yes, for the team only |
| Quiet for shift work | depends on other guests | easy to plan |
| Ideal for | individual assignments, short projects, rotating teams | fixed teams, long projects |

## What counts besides price

### Sleep and recovery

Installation work is physical, often with an early start. Anyone who sleeps badly works with less concentration – and in the end that costs more than a few euros' difference in accommodation. For shift work or very different working hours within the team, separate rooms or a separate apartment are a must.

### Team dynamics

Having your own apartment brings a team together: cooking together, going over the next day in the evening. At the same time, everyone needs a place to retreat to. The right mix is key – for example an apartment with single and double rooms.

### Flexibility when the crew changes

If workers rotate weekly or specialists only come for individual days, [workers' rooms](/monteurzimmer-augsburg/) that can be booked individually are more flexible. With an apartment, you pay even if a bed stays empty.

### Food costs

All Monteurzimmer Augsburg accommodation has a fully equipped kitchen with hob, oven, microwave, fridge and crockery. Cooking for yourselves instead of eating at restaurants and snack bars quickly saves several hundred euros per person on long stays.

### Parking

If the team arrives with several vans or trailers, parking should be sorted in advance. At Monteurzimmer Augsburg, parking at the house is free; the workers' houses have plenty of parking spaces.

## Quick decision guide

- **1 person, up to a few weeks:** single room (from €25) or 1-person [studio apartment](/monteurapartment-augsburg/) (from €30)
- **2–3 people:** double or triple room (from €15–18 per person)
- **4–6 people, fixed crew:** 4- or 6-person apartment (from €60 or €90 per day)
- **7–16 people:** workers' house (from €120 to €200 per day)
- **up to 20 people:** 20-person accommodation (from €200 per day) or a combination of several units
- **changing crew:** individual workers' rooms

## Frequently asked questions: workers' room or workers' apartment?

### From how many people is a workers' apartment worth it?

Based on Monteurzimmer Augsburg's prices, from around four people: a 4-person apartment costs from €60 per day – exactly the same as four beds in a multi-bed room at €15 each.

### Which is cheaper, a workers' room or a workers' apartment?

For 1 to 3 people, the workers' room is cheaper. From four people, the apartment and the multi-bed room are level, and for large teams a workers' house is the cheapest per person.

### Do I have to pay for every bed in a workers' apartment?

The price of a workers' apartment applies per apartment per day. Empty beds do not reduce the price – which is why it is best suited to fixed teams.

### Do workers' rooms have a kitchen too?

Yes, at Monteurzimmer Augsburg all accommodation has a fully equipped kitchen.

### Is there a discount for long stays?

Yes, Monteurzimmer Augsburg offers special rates for long-term stays. Just mention it when you enquire.
