> **In short:** If you are in Augsburg with a van or company vehicle: parking in the city centre is mostly paid, while in the outer districts parking is usually easier. The key rules are set out in the German Road Traffic Regulations (StVO): vehicles over **7.5 t** and trailers over **2 t** may not be parked regularly in residential areas at night (10 pm–6 am) or on Sundays and public holidays, and pavement parking – where permitted – is only allowed up to **2.8 t**. If you are working on an installation job in Augsburg, you need two parking spaces every day: one at the site and one at your accommodation. With a little preparation, both can be organised without stress. This guide sums up the rules that really matter for vans, Sprinters and trailers, and shows what you should clarify before you arrive.

## The key parking rules for vans and trailers

| Vehicle | Rule | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Cars and vans up to 3.5 t | may generally park wherever parking is permitted | StVO |
| Motor vehicles over 7.5 t, trailers over 2 t | no regular parking 10 pm–6 am or on Sundays and public holidays in purely and generally residential areas, special recreational areas, spa and hospital areas | § 12 Abs. 3a StVO |
| Trailers without a towing vehicle | may be left for a maximum of two weeks at a time | § 12 Abs. 3b StVO |
| Pavement parking | only where permitted by signs and only up to 2.8 t gross vehicle weight | Sign 315 (Zeichen 315) StVO |

Also watch the height: many multi-storey car parks and some park-and-ride facilities are designed for cars. High-roof vans often do not fit under the entrance barrier. Check the stated clearance height before you drive in.

## City centre, residents' parking and car parks

Parking in Augsburg city centre is largely managed and paid: you park with a parking ticket, via mobile phone parking (Handy-Parken) or in a multi-storey car park. Several neighbourhoods have residents' parking zones (Bewohnerparken) where, without a residents' parking permit (Bewohnerparkausweis), you may only park for a limited time or not at all – the signs on site are what counts. For longer jobs in the old town (Altstadt), it is worth checking with your client whether a parking space on the property or a permit is available.

In the outer districts and in industrial estates, parking is generally more relaxed. On the edge of the city there are park-and-ride facilities with connections to trams and buses.

## Parking at the site: apply for a no-stopping zone in good time

If you need to load and unload directly in front of the site, you will need a **permit**. Temporary no-stopping zones (Halteverbotszone) are applied for at the responsible authority of the City of Augsburg. Because the signs usually have to be put up a few days in advance, you should apply early. Clarify with your client who will take care of this – it has often already been done.

Site checklist:

- Is there a parking space on the property?
- Has a no-stopping zone been applied for – and for what period?
- Where may trailers, skips or materials be left?
- Are there access restrictions, such as clearance heights or weight limits?

## Low-emission zone and driving bans

Before you travel, check the current rules of the City of Augsburg on the low-emission zone (Umweltzone) and any requirement for an emissions sticker (Umweltplakette). Especially with older vans or vehicles registered abroad, it is worth checking the city's website before the crew sets off.

## Accommodation with parking: what companies should look out for

Parking at the accommodation is often forgotten when booking – and then causes trouble on the evening of arrival. Ask in advance:

1. Is parking **included in the price** or does it cost extra?
2. Will **vans and trailers** fit in the parking area?
3. How many vehicles can the team park **at the same time**?
4. Does anything need to be **arranged** for large vehicles?

## Tips to avoid parking tickets and towing costs

- **Take the signs seriously:** Temporary no-stopping zones at construction sites apply from the time stated – for tradespeople too.
- **Always keep fire brigade access routes and entrances and exits clear**, even if it is only "for five minutes".
- **Keep an eye on your parking time:** Mobile phone parking reminds you in time before it runs out and can be extended.
- **Do not leave tools visible in the vehicle,** especially overnight and at weekends.
- **Do not leave trailers on public roads for more than two weeks.**

## Alternatives to the van

Not everyone has to drive to the site in their own vehicle every day. Augsburg has a dense tram and bus network, and public transport is close to our accommodation. A model many teams use: one van takes the materials to the site, and the rest of the team follows by bus and tram. This saves time looking for parking and saves money.

## Frequently asked questions about parking in Augsburg

### Can I park my van in residential areas?

Vans up to 3.5 t may park wherever parking is permitted. For motor vehicles over 7.5 t and trailers over 2 t, parking in residential areas is prohibited from 10 pm to 6 am and on Sundays and public holidays (§ 12 Abs. 3a StVO).

### How long can a trailer be left at the roadside?

A trailer without a towing vehicle may be left in public parking areas for a maximum of two weeks at a time.

### Does Monteurzimmer Augsburg have parking for company vehicles?

Yes, parking right at the house is free. Tell us the number and size of your vehicles when you enquire.

### How do I get a no-stopping zone in front of the construction site?

You apply for it at the responsible authority of the City of Augsburg. Apply early, because the signs have to be put up in advance.

### Does my vehicle need an emissions sticker?

That depends on the current rules of the City of Augsburg. Check the latest situation on the city's website before you travel.
