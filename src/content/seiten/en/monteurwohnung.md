## Why a workers' apartment instead of individual rooms?

You rent a workers' apartment **exclusively for your team**. No strangers share the kitchen, bathroom or living area. On a day-to-day installation job, that has real advantages:

- **Peace and predictability:** your team decides for itself when to cook, do laundry and sleep – important for shift work or early starts on site.
- **Shared kitchen:** cooking for yourselves instead of eating out every day cuts food costs considerably.
- **Fixed price per day:** the price applies to the apartment, not per head. With a full team, the price per person drops – in the 20-person accommodation to an effective €10 per person per day.
- **Team cohesion:** plans for the next day can be sorted out in the evening around the kitchen table.

If you're travelling alone or in a pair, a [workers' room](/monteurzimmer-augsburg/) or a [contractor studio apartment](/monteurapartment-augsburg/) is often a better fit. You'll find a detailed comparison with worked examples in our guide [Workers' room or workers' apartment?](/blog/monteurzimmer-oder-monteurwohnung/).
