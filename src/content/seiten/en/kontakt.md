## What we need for a quick quote

The more complete your enquiry, the faster and more accurate our quote. The following details help us:

- **Company** (if you're booking for a business) and contact person with phone number
- **Number of people** and preferred allocation (single, double, shared rooms or a whole apartment)
- **Arrival date** and expected **length of stay**
- **Type of accommodation:** [workers' rooms](/monteurzimmer-augsburg/), [workers' apartment](/monteurwohnung-augsburg/), [contractor studio apartment](/monteurapartment-augsburg/) or [workers' house](/monteurhaus-augsburg/)
- **Vehicles:** number and size (car, van, trailer)
- **Special requirements:** late arrival, shift work, billing details

## Enquire by phone or by form?

For short-notice bookings – for example if your team is arriving tomorrow – it's best to call us directly on +49 821 419028-28. For projects you can plan ahead, the form is ideal: you have all the details in writing, and we'll get back to you with a quote as quickly as possible. You can also submit a detailed booking request via the [Booking](/reservierung-monterwohungaugsburg/) page.

## Where the accommodation is located

Our accommodation is spread across several locations in the city of Augsburg, including Barfüßerstraße 7 (city centre) and Stiermannstraße, where workers' rooms I–IV are located. From there, the A8 motorway (Munich–Stuttgart) and the B17 are quickly reached, and Messe Augsburg (the trade fair centre) is around 15 minutes away. Public transport, shops and restaurants are nearby.
