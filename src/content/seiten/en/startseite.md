Whether it's a single service engineer for three nights or an installation crew for several months: we offer four types of accommodation that differ in privacy, group size and price. All of them are fully furnished and ready to move into – you only need to bring your workwear and tools.

## Who are our workers' rooms for?

Our accommodation is designed for people who work in Augsburg and can't travel home every evening:

- **Fitters and service engineers** in plant construction, mechanical engineering and industrial construction
- **Tradespeople and construction crews** – from electricians and drywall installers to scaffolders
- **Exhibition stand builders and exhibitors** at [Messe Augsburg](/messe-monteurzimmer-augsburg/) (the Augsburg trade fair centre)
- **Temporary staffing agencies and recruitment firms** arranging accommodation for their employees
- **Project teams and business travellers** looking for an affordable alternative to a hotel

## How booking works for companies

1. **Send an enquiry:** via the [booking form](/reservierung-monterwohungaugsburg/) or by phone on +49 821 419028-28. It helps if you tell us your company name, number of people, preferred room allocation, arrival date and expected length of stay.
2. **Receive a quote:** we'll get back to you as quickly as possible with a specific proposal – room, apartment or house – and the price for your dates.
3. **Confirm:** once you accept, the accommodation is reserved. It's best to give us your billing address, cost centre or project number at the same time, so the invoice is right first time.
4. **Arrive and get to work:** check-in from 3:00 pm; if you arrive late in the evening or early in the morning, we'll arrange the key handover with you in advance.
5. **Pay:** by bank transfer or in cash.

## Location and transport links

Our accommodation is spread across **several locations in the city of Augsburg**, including **Barfüßerstraße 7** (city centre, also studio apartments) and **Stiermannstraße** (workers' rooms I–IV). From our properties you can quickly reach the main work sites:

- **A8 motorway** (Munich–Stuttgart) – fast access via the Augsburg junctions
- **B17** – the north–south trunk road in the west of Augsburg, towards Landsberg am Lech
- **Messe Augsburg** – around 15 minutes
- **Public transport** – tram and bus stops nearby, Augsburg Hauptbahnhof (central station) with long-distance and regional trains
- **Shops and restaurants** in the area

Augsburg is a convenient base for jobs in all of its districts – such as Oberhausen, Lechhausen, Kriegshaber, Pfersee, Göggingen or Haunstetten – as well as in surrounding towns like Gersthofen, Neusäß, Friedberg or Königsbrunn.

Check-in is from 3:00 pm, check-out is by 10:00 am. Flexible times are possible by arrangement.
