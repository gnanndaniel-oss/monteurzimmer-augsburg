## Who is a contractor studio apartment suitable for?

The contractor studio apartment sits between a single room and a large apartment:

- **Individual fitters and service technicians** staying for several weeks who want their own self-contained accommodation
- **Teams of two**, e.g. a fitter and an assistant, who don't want to share with other guests
- **Small teams of up to 4 people** who want to cook together and have some peace and quiet in the evening
- **Project managers, engineers and business travellers** who need somewhere to work and unwind after hours

If your team has more than four people, a [workers' apartment](/monteurwohnung-augsburg/) or a [workers' house](/monteurhaus-augsburg/) is usually cheaper per head. If you only need a bed and want to save money, our [workers' rooms](/monteurzimmer-augsburg/) start at €15 per person per night.

All prices vary depending on season and occupancy; special rates are available for long-term stays.
