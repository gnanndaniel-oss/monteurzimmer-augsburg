## Why a whole house for your installation team?

On large projects with many workers, a normal apartment soon reaches its limits: too few bathrooms, too small a kitchen, not enough parking. A workers' house solves these problems:

- **One house, one team:** no outside guests, clear responsibility and simple organisation
- **Plenty of parking** for company vehicles right outside the house
- **Garden and barbecue area** – after long working days, a real boost for team morale
- **Shared kitchen:** self-catering noticeably cuts expenses
- **Low cost per head:** at full occupancy, the 16-person house works out at €12.50 per person per day

## Facilities

- Fully equipped kitchen with hob, oven, microwave, fridge and crockery
- Free high-speed Wi-Fi and flat-screen TV
- Washing machine, tumble dryer on request
- Garden with barbecue area, parking at the house
- Optional extras: bed linen and towels changed weekly, and weekly cleaning

## Which size is right?

For 7 people, the 7-person house from €120 per day is enough. Teams of 8 to 13 people are well accommodated in the 13-person house from €175, and up to 16 people in the 16-person house from €200 per day. For even larger teams there is accommodation for 20 people from €200 per day (see [workers' apartment](/monteurwohnung-augsburg/)) – or we combine several units. Smaller groups will find suitable [workers' rooms](/monteurzimmer-augsburg/) from €12 per person per night.

All prices are starting prices and vary depending on season and occupancy. We offer special rates for longer stays.
