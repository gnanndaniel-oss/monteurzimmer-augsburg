## Who is unit IV suitable for?

With room for up to 12 people, unit IV is the largest of the four units – made for large installation teams, construction gangs or exhibition-build crews who want to stay together. For 10 or more people in one apartment, our lowest price of from €12 per person per night applies – which makes renting the complete unit IV especially cost-effective for a team of 10 to 12 people.

## Facilities

Free high-speed Wi-Fi, a flat-screen TV in every room, a fully equipped kitchen with hob, oven, microwave, fridge and crockery, washing machine (tumble dryer on request); bed linen and towels changed weekly, and weekly cleaning, are available as optional extras. From Stiermannstraße you can quickly reach the A8 motorway and the B17, and Messe Augsburg (the trade fair centre) in around 15 minutes.
