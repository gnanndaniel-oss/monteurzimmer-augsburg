## Who is unit III suitable for?

Unit III is let as a shared flat for up to 7 people: rooms are let individually, and the kitchen and bathroom are shared. This suits solo travellers and small teams who only need one or two rooms particularly well. For solo travellers the shared-flat unit is ideal: your own room, a low price and a fully equipped shared kitchen.

## Facilities

Free high-speed Wi-Fi, a flat-screen TV in every room, a fully equipped kitchen with hob, oven, microwave, fridge and crockery, washing machine (tumble dryer on request), bed linen and towels changed weekly, and weekly cleaning. From Stiermannstraße you can quickly reach the A8 motorway and the B17, and Messe Augsburg (the trade fair centre) in around 15 minutes.
