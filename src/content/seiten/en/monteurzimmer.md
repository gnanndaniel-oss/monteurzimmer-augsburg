## Single room, double room or shared room?

- **Single room** – for foremen, site managers and anyone who needs peace and quiet after a shift or still has reports to write in the evening. From €25 per night.
- **Double room** – for well-established two-person teams, e.g. a service engineer with an assistant. From €18 per person.
- **Triple and shared rooms** – the most affordable option for crews who arrive together and work together. From €15 per person.
- **Whole unit** – with 10 or more people in one apartment, the price drops to from €12 per person.

## Frequently asked questions about our workers' rooms

### How many people fit in the workers' rooms?

When rented as a whole, the four units sleep up to 6 (ST5-100), 7 (ST5-200), 7 (ST5-300) and 12 people (ST5-400). Individual rooms can be booked as single, double or triple rooms.

### Can I book a single room for just one night?

Yes, you can book from just one night.

### What's the difference between "as an apartment" and "as a flat share"?

As an apartment, you rent the entire unit exclusively for your team. With the flat-share option (ST5-300), rooms are let individually and the kitchen and bathroom are shared.

### Is cleaning included in the price?

Yes, the accommodation is cleaned weekly, and bed linen and towels are changed weekly.

### Is there parking for vans?

Free parking is available right outside the building. If you have several or large vehicles, please let us know the number and size in advance.

### Is there a discount for long stays?

Yes, we offer special rates for longer stays. Just mention it when you send your enquiry.

### Can I arrive late in the evening?

Standard check-in is from 3:00 pm. Later arrival times are possible by arrangement – please let us know in advance.

### How far is it to Messe Augsburg?

Messe Augsburg is around 15 minutes from our accommodation.

### How can I pay?

By bank transfer or in cash.
